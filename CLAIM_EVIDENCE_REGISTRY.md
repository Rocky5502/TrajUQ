## Firewall notes

- The authoritative positive empirical claim is currently RQ1 only.
- RQ1 family-level values support heterogeneity wording as point estimates; they do not authorize family-specific significance claims because the aggregate supplies no family-specific intervals.
- RQ2--RQ5 progress, campaign status, frozen protocols, request counts, or near-complete registries do not authorize outcome claims.
- Cross-regime synthesis is descriptive only. The compatibility contract forbids raw cross-protocol AUROC rankings, pooled cross-regime significance tests, and causal observability-to-performance claims.
- Promotion rule: a row may change from `NOT YET AUTHORIZED` only after its named final artifact exists, passes its frozen audit/completeness/claim gate, and is connected to generated LaTeX without hand-entered empirical numbers.
